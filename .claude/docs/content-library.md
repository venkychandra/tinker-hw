## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Adventurous yet approachable** - Emphasizes personal discovery without intimidation
- **Expert and trustworthy** - 40+ years experience, ABTA/ATOL bonded
- **Warm and personal** - Focus on individual experience, not mass tourism
- **Quality-focused** - Hand-picked hotels, fine food & wine, award-winning routes

### Tone
Professional but warm; emphasizes experiential travel over luxury; encourages active discovery

### Key Verbs
Uncover, discover, explore, enjoy, experience, tailor-make

### What They Are
Self-guided specialists, active holiday experts, route planners

### What They're Not
Generic tour operators, resort hotels, passive tourism, budget travel
